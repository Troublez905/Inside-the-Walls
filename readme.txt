PROJECT STATUS
Pre-production. The immediate goal is one focused, working vertical slice rather than the entire planned game.

THE GAME
Up to 50 human players share a living correctional institution. AI-controlled inmates, correctional officers, and specialist staff fill empty roles so schedules and services continue below capacity.

Players enter as either a newly arrived inmate or a probationary correctional officer. Inmates learn routines, receive housing and work assignments, build relationships, and choose between legitimate development, social influence, and risky underground activity. Officers learn posts and procedures, supervise movement, respond to incidents, build rapport, and earn promotions toward institutional leadership.

Relationships, favors, debts, grievances, discipline, employment, rank, reputation, and transfers persist across sessions. Returning to a previous facility may reunite a player with characters who remember past choices.

FIRST PLAYABLE VERSION
- One small low-security prison
- Third-person movement and camera
- Inmate and officer role selection
- Intake and shift briefing
- One housing unit, dining hall, yard, officer station, medical room, classroom, workshop, commissary, and visiting room
- Counts, meals, work, recreation, and lockdown
- One inmate job and one officer duty
- AI inmates and staff
- One social interaction and one minor incident
- Reporting, review, and persistent consequences
- Save, disconnect, reconnect, and correct state restoration
- Initial two-player testing followed by 8-12 clients

PLANNED DIRECTION
- Dedicated, server-authoritative networking
- Up to 50 players per persistent prison
- Multiple security levels and special-purpose facilities
- Separate inmate and officer progression systems
- Relationship memory, reputation, economy, inventory, discipline, classification, transfers, and promotions
- Stylized-realistic graphics with grounded architecture and restrained gore
- Respectful character customization without identity-based statistics, morality, hostility, or automatic faction membership
